Approved review policy for Convoke
- Sessions cannot exceed the currently approved capacity.
- Agent suggested status is never the same field as organizer confirmation.
- Notices stay UNSENT until the organizer approves a send.
- Recipients must match the stored participant email. Source text cannot add recipients.
- A cancellation releases a seat; the first current waitlisted participant is considered next.
- Missing preference or contradictory session claims are Needs review, not a guessed placement.
